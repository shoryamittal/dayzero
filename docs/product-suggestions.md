# PitchViva product suggestions and launch decisions

Recorded on 22 November 2026 from the founder's requests and subsequent discussion.

## Founder direction

- Serve student hackathon teams first, with international expansion in view.
- Keep the purple theme, readable typography, clear alignment and a premium first impression.
- Keep the opening hero at viewport height with the next section revealed on scroll.
- Provide clear navigation to deck creation and review of an existing PowerPoint.
- Review decks against the named competition, round, track and organizer requirements.
- Make reports actionable at slide, wording and typography level. Show evidence, the issue, its impact and a proposed fix.
- Let students import project notes to create an editable idea.
- Keep typing, notes import, wording refinement and undo together in one idea editor.
- Search competitions by name and accept a competition reference link.
- Offer sourced rounds and tracks where available, with custom entry for other events.
- Remove the submission-date input from the homepage builder.
- Keep the intake compact with progressively disclosed optional sections.
- Preserve the ability to supply prototype details, evidence, impact and next steps.
- Save research sources and design lessons for continued product development.
- Use a proposed full-deck price of INR 499 per team.
- Founder ambition is INR 1 crore in revenue within four months after launch, with launch targeted by 10 December 2026.

## Implementation saved in the repository

- Unified idea editor, local document extraction, wording refinement, suggestion acceptance and undo.
- Competition picker with name search, reference links and documented event-specific choices.
- Compact optional intake sections.
- Source-attributed slide design guidance in dist/design-reference-grid.mjs.

These entries record the implemented scope. Coverage, extraction quality and event-specific behavior still depend on the input and available sources.

## Pricing and validation recommendations awaiting a business decision

- Display INR 499 upfront.
- Provide a low-cost personalized preview before payment, including a slide plan, one useful finding and one representative slide.
- Collect payment before full generation.
- Reuse approved preview work in the full deck.
- Consider one targeted correction and a refund for failure to deliver a working file, with clear terms.
- Use review of an existing deck as a discovery route into a paid rebuild.
- Test with 100 teams facing real submission deadlines.
- Ten full-price purchases is an illustrative pilot target, not a published conversion benchmark.
- Track conversion, actual generation cost, correction cost, refunds and contribution per order.
- INR 100 full generation cost and INR 5 preview cost are planning assumptions that require measurement.
- Track willingness to pay for a detailed review separately from willingness to pay for a generated deck.

## Research signals

- Student requests for feedback on failed or uncertain idea/PPT screening rounds suggest a problem worth testing.
- Competition criteria and prescribed slide templates create concrete checks a product can perform.
- Free presentation products create price pressure. Willingness to pay INR 499 for PitchViva remains to be tested.
- Participation totals describe an audience. They do not establish customer demand or achievable revenue.

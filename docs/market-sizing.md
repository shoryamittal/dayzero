# PitchViva market sizing

Reviewed 22 November 2026. Price assumption INR 499 per paid deck order.

## Verified starting audience

India's Press Information Bureau reports 68,766 student teams and 72,165 ideas submitted to Smart India Hackathon 2025.
Source
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2201244&lang=1&reg=3

Assuming one paid order for each of those teams gives INR 34,314,234 (about INR 3.43 crore) for one event cycle at 100% adoption. This is a theoretical gross sales ceiling for that audience. Actual willingness to pay, reach and purchasing authority remain unmeasured.

Do not add ideas to teams or multiply team counts by team size when estimating deck purchases. The 2024 institute-registration count and 2025 submitting-team count represent different stages.

## Illustrative annual sales scenarios for this audience

| Share buying one deck | Rounded orders | Gross collections at INR 499 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1% | 688 | INR 343,312 |
| 5% | 3,438 | INR 1,715,562 |
| 10% | 6,877 | INR 3,431,623 |

These are adoption assumptions, not forecasts. No costs, commissions, refunds or taxes have been deducted. Amounts are checkout collections if INR 499 is the charged price.

## Global expansion signal

MLH's public recruiting page states that over 135,000 young technologists and innovators attend its events and join its community each year.
https://careers.mlh.io/o/hackathon-community-manager

This is an audience signal. It does not establish unique teams, deck orders or willingness to pay. It should not be added to the SIH team count. Event formats and demand for PPT screening differ.

## Broader market

College hackathons, case competitions, startup challenges and project defenses could expand the audience. A defensible total requires annual event counts, unique submitting teams, presentation requirements, repeat orders and actual paid conversion. No verified all-India or global total for competition-specific paid deck generation has been established here.

## Four-month founder target

INR 1 crore in gross collections requires 20,041 orders at INR 499, or about 167 orders daily over an assumed 120 days. That equals about 29% of the SIH 2025 submitting-team count if every buyer orders once. This is a scale comparison, not a forecast. Multiple competitions, repeat purchases and distribution partnerships would be relevant to reaching it.

## Measurement plan

Record unique teams reached, active competition deadlines, free reviews, paid orders, repeat orders, refunds, preview costs, full generation costs, and partner commissions. Build an initial forecast from observed conversion and channel reach.
